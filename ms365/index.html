Microsoft 365 AI

Transform daily productivity with Microsoft 365 Copilot — AI assistance embedded in Teams, Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, and SharePoint.

🔍 What Is Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant deeply integrated into the Microsoft 365 suite of applications. Powered by large language models (GPT-4 class) combined with your organization's own data through Microsoft Graph, Copilot can summarize meetings, draft documents, analyze spreadsheets, answer emails, and much more — all within the apps you already use daily.

Unlike a standalone AI chatbot, M365 Copilot has context: it knows your calendar, emails, documents, chats, and meetings — and uses that context to provide highly relevant, personalized assistance.

💡 Microsoft Graph is the unified API that connects M365 Copilot to your organization's data across all Microsoft 365 apps — securely and with your existing permissions.
⚠️ M365 Copilot requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license (add-on) in addition to your standard M365 subscription. Check with your IT admin for availability.

👤 Who Is It For?

Knowledge Workers

Draft faster, summarize meetings, and find information across all M365 apps.

Managers & Leaders

Get meeting summaries, action items, and status updates without attending every call.

Analysts

Use Copilot in Excel to analyze data, spot trends, and generate formulas in plain English.

IT Admins

Configure Copilot policies, manage permissions, and monitor usage via Admin Center.

📚 Key Concepts — Copilot Per App

📧 Copilot in Outlook

  • Draft emails from bullet points or short prompts
  • Summarize long email threads in seconds
  • Adjust tone (professional, casual, concise) with one click
  • Coaching tips to improve message clarity and impact

📝 Copilot in Word

  • Draft entire documents from a brief prompt or outline
  • Rewrite, summarize, or expand selected text
  • Extract key points and generate executive summaries
  • Pull in data from referenced files, emails, and meetings

📊 Copilot in Excel

  • Ask questions about your data in plain English ("Show me sales by region last quarter")
  • Generate formulas and explain what they do
  • Identify trends, outliers, and correlations automatically
  • Create charts and pivot tables from natural language prompts

🎯 Copilot in Teams

  • Real-time meeting transcription and smart summaries
  • Action item extraction and follow-up suggestions post-meeting
  • Catch-up summaries for channels you missed
  • Draft replies and messages in chat with AI assistance

📊 Copilot in PowerPoint

  • Create full presentations from a text prompt or Word document
  • Suggest slide designs and layouts
  • Summarize or reorganize existing decks
  • Add speaker notes automatically

🔍 Microsoft Copilot Chat (BizChat)

  • Web-grounded AI chat with enterprise data via Graph
  • Ask questions that span multiple M365 apps ("Summarize emails and Teams messages about Project X from this week")
  • Available at copilot.microsoft.com and in Teams sidebar
  • Work Data mode (protected) vs. Web mode (public web)

Microsoft Graph — The Foundation

  • Unified API connecting all M365 user and organizational data
  • Respects existing permission model — Copilot only sees what you can see
  • Enables semantic search across emails, documents, chats, and calendar

🚀 Quick-Start Tips

  1. Start with Outlook: Use "Draft with Copilot" on your next email — type 3 bullet points and let Copilot write the full message. Edit and send.
  2. Summarize Your Next Meeting in Teams: Enable Copilot in Teams Settings → Meetings. After the call, open the Copilot recap tab for a full summary and action items.
  3. Ask Excel a Question: Open any spreadsheet, click the Copilot icon, and type "What are my top 5 items by revenue?" — no formulas needed.
  4. Use Copilot Chat for Cross-App Search: In Teams, open Copilot and ask "What are the main decisions from my last 3 meetings?" to see cross-app intelligence.
  5. Complete the Microsoft Adoption Scenarios: Visit adoption.microsoft.com/copilot to find use-case playbooks for your specific job role.

✍️ Writing Better Copilot Prompts

The quality of Copilot's output depends heavily on how you prompt it. Follow this framework:

Element What It Means Example
Goal What do you want Copilot to do? Draft an email
Context Relevant background information To a client who missed our deadline
Source Data or files to reference Using the project timeline in /Project-Plan.docx
Expectations Format, tone, length Keep it professional, under 150 words, with a solution
✅ Good prompt example: "Draft a professional email to our client explaining we'll be 2 days late on the Q1 report delivery. Reference the reasons from last week's Teams meeting. Keep it concise, empathetic, and include a new delivery date of Friday."
